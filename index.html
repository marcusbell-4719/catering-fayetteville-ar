How should lunch be timed around patient schedules?

Work backward from the first afternoon appointment. Many practices eat in shifts, so the last nurse may reach the table well after the first. That gap is where food safety matters.

The USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service says bacteria grow most rapidly between 40°F and 140°F, doubling in as little as 20 minutes. FSIS advises never leaving food out more than 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F. The FDA Food Code 2022 sets hot holding at 135°F or above.

Set a clear cutoff for the break room. Rx Catering NWA's kitchen hours are Monday to Friday, 6:30 AM to 5 PM, and Saturday, 7 AM to 3 PM.

How do dietary counts work?

Count before ordering, not after delivery. The Celiac Disease Foundation puts celiac disease at about 1 in 100 people worldwide. Gallup found in 2023 that 4% of U.S. adults call themselves vegetarian and 1% vegan. In a clinic of 25, one or two special plates is a realistic plan.

Rx Catering NWA marks gluten-free hot entrees GF; Baked Tilapia and Pot Roast are both gluten-free. Its vegetarian entrees are Veggie Lasagna, Bean Enchiladas, and Stuffed Portabella Mushrooms. Vegan, paleo, organic, kosher, and halal menus sit on rxcatering.net, and every dish there can be ordered and delivered. Organic ingredients can be sourced on request.
